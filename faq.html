Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Uplyncio marketplace

Buyer's Questions
How do I get started as a buyer?+
Create a free buyer account, browse our marketplace of 20,000+ publisher sites, filter by DA, DR, niche, country, and price. When you find a site you like, click "Order" and fill in your target URL, anchor text, and content preferences. The publisher will receive your order and start working on it.
What is Guest Posting (Article Posting)?+
Guest posting means publishing an article on another website that includes a backlink to your site. You can either provide your own article or have the publisher write one for you. It's one of the most effective ways to build high-quality backlinks and improve your search engine rankings.
What is Link Insertion (Niche Edit)?+
Link insertion means adding your backlink to an existing, already-indexed article on a publisher's website. This is faster than guest posting because no new content needs to be created, and the page may already have authority and traffic.
What does each order status mean?+
Pending: Order placed, waiting for publisher to accept.
Accepted: Publisher is working on your order.
Delivered: Content is live, waiting for your approval.
Completed: You approved the delivery, order is done.
Rejected: Publisher declined the order, funds refunded.
Cancelled: Order was cancelled, funds refunded.
How long does delivery take?+
Most orders are delivered within 7–14 business days. Each publisher shows their estimated TAT (Turnaround Time) on their site listing. You can filter sites by TAT to find faster publishers.
What is DA, DR, and Traffic?+
DA (Domain Authority): Moz's metric (0–100) predicting how well a site will rank. Higher = better.
DR (Domain Rating): Ahrefs' metric (0–100) measuring the strength of a site's backlink profile.
Traffic: Estimated monthly organic visitors from search engines, provided by Ahrefs.
What is the Completion Rate?+
Completion Rate shows what percentage of orders a publisher has successfully completed. A higher completion rate means the publisher is more reliable. We recommend choosing publishers with 70%+ completion rate.
What is TAT (Turnaround Time)?+
TAT is the estimated number of business days a publisher needs to complete your order. Shorter TAT means faster delivery. You can filter sites by TAT in the marketplace.
Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?+
Yes. If a publisher fails to deliver within the deadline, or if the delivered content doesn't meet the agreed requirements, you can request a revision or cancel the order for a full refund. We also offer a 12-month link guarantee — if the link is removed within 12 months, you get a replacement or refund.
How do I add orders to the Cart?+
When browsing the marketplace, click the shopping cart icon on any site card to add it to your cart. You can review all items in your cart, adjust quantities, and checkout when ready.
What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept PayPal, credit/debit cards (via Stripe), bank transfers, and USDT TRC20 cryptocurrency. You can add funds to your account balance and use it to place orders.
What is the difference between Dofollow and Nofollow links?+
Dofollow: Passes SEO authority (link juice) to your website, helping it rank higher.
Nofollow: Doesn't pass SEO authority but still drives traffic and looks natural in your backlink profile.
Most buyers prefer dofollow links for SEO impact.
What are Campaigns and Bulk Orders?+
Campaigns: Set a monthly budget and link goal, and we automatically find and place orders matching your criteria on autopilot.
Bulk Orders: Upload a CSV file with multiple sites and order details to place many orders at once, saving time.
How do I search and filter sites?+
Use the search bar to find sites by domain or keyword. Use filters like Price, DA, DR, Traffic, Country, Language, Category, Link Type, and more to narrow down results. Click "More filters" for advanced options like Completion Rate and TAT.
What guarantees do you provide?+
We offer a 12-month link guarantee. If your link is removed within 12 months of placement, we will provide a replacement link on a similar quality site or issue a full refund. We also guarantee content quality — if the published article doesn't match agreed specifications, you can request a revision.
Publisher's Questions
How do I become a publisher?+
Create a free publisher account, then add your website(s) to the marketplace. You can add sites one by one or upload in bulk using our CSV template. Once your site is approved, buyers can start ordering guest posts on it.
How do I add my websites?+
Go to your Publisher Dashboard → Click "Add or update websites" → You can add sites one at a time, or use the "Upload via file" tab to bulk upload using our Google Sheets template. Fill in your site URL, DA, DR, price, categories, and other details.
How much can I earn as a publisher?+
Earnings depend on your site's quality. Sites with DA 30+ typically earn $20–$100 per guest post, while DA 60+ sites can earn $200–$500+. You set your own prices. Our platform takes a small commission, and you receive the rest directly to your balance.
How do I get paid?+
When a buyer approves your delivery, the payment is released to your Uplyncio balance. You can withdraw your balance via PayPal, bank transfer, or USDT. Minimum withdrawal is $50.
What should I do when I receive an order?+
Review the order details (target URL, anchor text, content requirements). Accept the order if you can fulfill it, or reject with a reason if you can't. Once accepted, write/publish the content and submit the live URL as delivery.
What are the publisher requirements?+
Your site must have real organic traffic, original content, and a clean backlink profile. We don't accept PBN sites, sites with spammy content, or sites with excessive ads. Your site should have at least DA 10+ and some organic traffic.
How do I get verified as a publisher?+
Complete 10+ orders with good reviews, maintain a high completion rate (80%+), and keep your response time under 48 hours. Verified publishers get a badge, higher visibility in search results, and more orders.
Account & General
Can I change my password?+
Yes. Go to Account Settings in your dashboard and click "Change Password". You'll need to enter your current password and a new password that meets our security requirements (8+ characters, uppercase, lowercase, number, special character).
How do I manage my notification settings?+
Go to Account Settings → Notifications tab. You can enable/disable email notifications and WhatsApp notifications for order updates, new messages, and marketing communications.
Can I have both a buyer and publisher account?+
Yes! You can switch between buyer and publisher roles from your dashboard. Use the "Switch to Publisher" or "Switch to Buyer" option in your sidebar menu.
How do I delete my account?+
Go to Account Settings and click "Delete Account". Please note that this action is permanent and cannot be undone. Make sure to withdraw any remaining balance before deleting your account.
Is buying backlinks safe for SEO?+
When done correctly, yes. We focus on editorial, content-based placements on real websites with real traffic — not spammy link farms. Our approach mimics natural guest blogging, which is a widely accepted practice. We recommend building links gradually and diversifying your anchor text.
How do I contact support?+
You can reach us through the chat widget (Max — AI Assistant) on any page, or email us at info@uplyncio.com. We typically respond within 24 hours.

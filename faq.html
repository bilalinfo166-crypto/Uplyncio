Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using the Uplyncio marketplace

Getting Started
How do I create an account on Uplyncio?+
Go to uplyncio.com and click 'Get Started' for a buyer account or 'Become Publisher' for a publisher account. Fill in your Full Name, Email, and Password. Complete the Cloudflare captcha and click the signup button. You'll receive a 6-digit OTP verification code on your email. Enter the code to activate your account and access your dashboard.
I didn't receive my verification code (OTP). What should I do?+
Check your spam/junk folder — the email comes from info@uplyncio.com with subject 'Verify your Uplyncio account'. If it's not there, click 'Resend Code' on the verification screen (available after 60 seconds). Make sure your email address is correct — a typo will send the code to the wrong address. If you still don't receive it, try signing up with a different email or contact us at info@uplyncio.com.
How do I sign in to my account?+
Go to uplyncio.com and click 'Sign In' in the top right. Choose whether you're signing in as a Publisher or Buyer. Enter your registered email and password. You can also sign in with Google by clicking 'Continue with Google'.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?+
On the login screen, click 'Forgot Password?'. Enter your registered email address. We'll send you a password reset link. Click the link in the email and set a new password. Your new password must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, number, and special character.
Can I sign up with Google?+
Yes! Click 'Continue with Google' on the signup or login screen. Select your Google account and authorize Uplyncio. Your account will be created automatically — no OTP verification needed for Google signups.
Can I have both a buyer and publisher account?+
Yes! You can switch between buyer and publisher roles from your dashboard sidebar. Click 'Switch to Publisher' or 'Switch to Buyer' in the menu. Both roles share the same login credentials.
Buyer — Browsing & Ordering
How do I browse and find sites?+
Log in to your buyer dashboard. You'll see the marketplace with all available publisher sites. Use the search bar to find sites by domain or keyword. Use filters (Price, DA, DR, Traffic, Country, Language, Category, Link Type) to narrow results. Click 'More filters' for advanced options like Completion Rate, TAT, and Sort By.
What is the difference between 'Verified Sites' and 'All Sites'?+
Verified Sites are from publishers who have completed 10+ orders with high ratings — these are the most reliable. All Sites shows every available site including newer publishers. We recommend starting with Verified Sites for the best experience.
How do I place an order?+
Find a site you like in the marketplace → Click 'Order' → Fill in your Target URL, Anchor Text, and any content requirements → Choose service type (Guest Post, Link Insertion, or Write & Publish) → Review the price → Click 'Confirm Order'. The publisher will receive your order notification via email.
What is Guest Posting (Article Posting)?+
Guest posting means publishing a new article on a publisher's website that includes a backlink to your site. You can either provide your own article or have the publisher write one. The article is published as a regular blog post on their site.
What is Link Insertion (Niche Edit)?+
Link insertion means adding your backlink to an existing, already-indexed article on the publisher's site. This is faster because no new content needs to be written, and the page already has authority and traffic from Google.
What is Write & Publish?+
With Write & Publish, the publisher writes the article for you based on your topic, keywords, and requirements, then publishes it with your backlink. This is the easiest option — you just provide your link details and they handle everything.
What does each order status mean?+
Pending: Order placed, waiting for publisher to accept.
Accepted: Publisher is working on your order.
Delivered: Content is live, review and approve it.
Completed: You approved, order is done, payment released to publisher.
Rejected: Publisher declined, your funds are refunded.
Cancelled: Order cancelled, funds refunded.
Revision: You requested changes, publisher is updating.
How long does delivery take?+
Most orders are delivered within 7–14 business days. Each site listing shows the publisher's TAT (Turnaround Time). Filter by TAT to find faster publishers. If a publisher doesn't deliver by the deadline, you can cancel for a full refund.
Can I request a revision?+
Yes! If the delivered content doesn't match your requirements, click 'Request Revision' on the delivered order. Explain what needs to be changed. The publisher will update the content. You get up to 2 free revisions per order.
How do I add orders to my cart?+
Click the cart icon on any site card in the marketplace to add it. View your cart from the top navigation. In the cart, review items, see the total price, and proceed to checkout.

What payment methods do you accept?+
We accept: PayPal (instant), Credit/Debit Cards via Stripe (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Bank Transfer (2-3 business days), and USDT TRC20 cryptocurrency (instant on-chain confirmation).
Is my payment secure?+
Yes. All payments are processed through trusted third-party processors (PayPal, Stripe). We never store your card details on our servers. Our site uses SSL encryption (HTTPS) and follows PCI DSS compliance through our payment partners.

Yes. You can get a refund if: the publisher rejects your order, the publisher doesn't deliver by the deadline, or the delivered content doesn't meet the agreed requirements after revisions. Refunds are credited to your Uplyncio balance. Contact info@uplyncio.com for refund to original payment method.
What do the different balance categories mean?+
Available Balance: Funds ready to use for orders.
Reserved: Funds locked for pending/active orders.
Total Balance: Available + Reserved combined.
Publisher — Adding Sites
How do I add my website as a publisher?+
Log in to your publisher dashboard → Click 'Add or update websites' → Agree to Terms & Conditions → Choose to add one website at a time or upload via file (CSV/Google Sheets). Fill in your site URL, DA, DR, traffic, pricing, categories, and requirements. Click Submit — your site will go live after review.
How do I bulk upload sites using the template?+
Go to Add websites → Click 'Upload via file' tab → Click 'Open Template' to open our Google Sheets template → Make a copy of the sheet → Fill in your sites data (URL, DA, DR, Price, Category, etc.) → Download as CSV → Upload the CSV file in the upload area. All sites will be added at once.
What information do I need to provide for each site?+
Required: Site URL, Guest Post Price. Recommended: DA, DR, Monthly Traffic, Categories (up to 5), Link Type (Dofollow/Nofollow), Language, Country, TAT (days), Word Count minimum, and any special Requirements for buyers.
How do I update my site's pricing or details?+
Go to your publisher dashboard → Find the site in 'My Platforms' → Click the edit icon → Update any fields → Save changes. Changes take effect immediately.
Why was my site rejected?+
Sites may be rejected if they: have very low DA (under 10), contain spammy or adult content, are PBN (Private Blog Network) sites, have no organic traffic, have excessive ads, or duplicate an already-listed site. You'll receive an email with the specific reason.
Publisher — Orders & Earnings
What do I do when I receive an order?+
You'll get an email notification. Log in → Go to 'Orders' → Review the order details (target URL, anchor text, requirements) → Click 'Accept' if you can fulfill it. Then write/publish the content and submit the live URL as delivery.
How do I deliver an order?+
After publishing the content with the buyer's backlink → Go to the order → Click 'Deliver' → Paste the live URL where the content is published → Submit. The buyer will review and approve it.
How much can I earn as a publisher?+
Earnings depend on your site quality:

DA 30-50: $50–$150 per guest post
DA 50-70: $150–$300 per guest post

When a buyer approves your delivery, the payment is released to your Uplyncio balance. You can withdraw via PayPal, Bank Transfer, or USDT. Minimum withdrawal is $50. Withdrawals are processed within 3-5 business days.
How do I withdraw my earnings?+
Go to your publisher dashboard → Click 'Balance' or 'Withdraw' → Choose withdrawal method (PayPal, Bank, USDT) → Enter the amount and payment details → Submit request. You'll receive the funds within 3-5 business days.
What happens if I can't fulfill an order?+
If you can't complete an order, click 'Reject' and provide a reason. The buyer will receive a full refund. Note: frequent rejections lower your completion rate, which affects your visibility in search results.
Publisher — Verification & Trust
How do I get verified as a publisher?+
Complete 10+ orders successfully with good ratings, maintain a completion rate above 80%, and keep your response time under 48 hours. Once eligible, you'll automatically receive the Verified badge. Verified publishers get higher visibility and more orders.
What does the Verified badge mean?+
The Verified badge means the publisher has: completed 10+ orders, maintained a high completion rate (80%+), consistently delivered quality work, and been vetted by the Uplyncio team. Buyers trust verified publishers more.
How does the Completion Rate work?+
Completion Rate = (Completed Orders ÷ Total Orders) × 100. It counts all orders you've accepted. Rejected orders before acceptance don't count. 80%+ is good, 90%+ is excellent. Higher rates mean better search ranking.
SEO & Links
What is DA (Domain Authority)?+
DA is Moz's metric (0–100) that predicts how well a website will rank in search results. It's based on the quality and quantity of backlinks pointing to the site. Higher DA = stronger site = more valuable backlink for you.
What is DR (Domain Rating)?+
DR is Ahrefs' metric (0–100) measuring the strength of a website's backlink profile. It considers the number and quality of referring domains. A higher DR generally means the site has more authoritative backlinks.
What is the difference between Dofollow and Nofollow links?+
Dofollow: Passes SEO authority (link juice) to your website, directly helping your rankings.
Nofollow: Doesn't pass SEO authority but still drives referral traffic and looks natural in your backlink profile.
Sponsored: Tells Google the link is a paid placement. Required by Google guidelines for paid links.
Most buyers prefer dofollow for maximum SEO impact.
Is buying backlinks safe for SEO?+
When done correctly through editorial, content-based placements on real websites with real traffic — yes. Our approach mimics natural guest blogging. We recommend: building links gradually (not all at once), diversifying anchor text (mix branded, generic, and exact match), choosing relevant niches, and targeting sites with real traffic.
What guarantees do you provide?+
We offer a 12-month link guarantee. If your backlink is removed within 12 months of placement, we provide a replacement link on a similar quality site or issue a full refund. We also guarantee content quality and offer revisions if needed.
What is Spam Score?+
Spam Score is Moz's metric (0–100) indicating how likely a site is to be penalized by search engines. Lower is better. We recommend choosing sites with Spam Score under 30. Sites with very high spam scores may do more harm than good to your SEO.

Campaigns & Bulk Orders
What are Campaigns?+
Campaigns let you set a monthly budget and link goal, choose your target niches and preferences, and let Uplyncio automatically find and place orders matching your criteria. It's like link building on autopilot — you set it once and it runs every month.
How do Bulk Orders work?+
Go to your buyer dashboard → Open Campaigns → Click 'Bulk Order (CSV)' → Download the CSV template → Fill in your sites (domain, target URL, anchor text) → Upload the CSV → Review the preview table with estimated prices → Click 'Submit'. All orders will be placed at once.
Can I pause or cancel a campaign?+
Yes. Go to Campaigns → Find your campaign → Click 'Pause' to temporarily stop it or 'Delete' to remove it entirely. Paused campaigns can be resumed anytime. Active orders from the campaign will continue to completion.
